About Us

Hyde Park–Waterhouse Corporation is a mission-driven disaster relief and infrastructure support company specializing in emergency response, restoration services, logistics operations, and community recovery.
We combine experienced personnel, operational expertise, and strategic partnerships to support disaster response efforts following:

  • Hurricanes
  • Tornadoes
  • Flooding
  • Severe storms
  • Infrastructure failures
  • Emergency events
  • Public facility disruptions

Our commitment is simple:
Respond quickly. Restore efficiently. Rebuild stronger.

Our Vision

To become a nationally recognized disaster recovery and infrastructure support organization known for operational excellence, community commitment, and resilient solutions.

Our Mission

To provide timely, professional, and compassionate disaster relief services that restore communities, support economic recovery, and strengthen America's infrastructure.
